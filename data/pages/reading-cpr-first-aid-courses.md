Welcome to Eastern PA Educators, your trusted source for CPR and first aid certification in the Reading PA area. Every day, situations arise where first aid skills can make a life-saving difference. From choking incidents at family dinners to falls at work, or car accidents on the road, emergencies can happen anywhere, anytime.

Our training programs are tailored for teachers, childcare providers, healthcare workers, and anyone who wants to be prepared for the unexpected. At Eastern PA Educators, we’re here to equip you with the skills and confidence to respond effectively to real-life situations. Whether it’s performing CPR, stabilizing an injury, or assisting someone in distress, your knowledge can turn a critical moment into a lifesaving one. Let us help you be ready to act when it matters most.

### 4 Reasons Why Our First Aid & CPR Training is Reading's Best

​

#### 1\. Experienced Trainers

Learn from certified, highly skilled instructors with years of hands-on experience in real-life emergencies. They combine technical expertise with practical insights, ensuring you're prepared to handle any situation. Our trainers also demonstrate the use of essential first aid equipment, such as AEDs (automated external defibrillators), EpiPens, and more.

​

#### 2\. Hands-On Learning with the Latest Tools

Practice makes perfect. That’s why our courses emphasize interactive, hands-on training using the latest first aid technologies and supplies. From practicing CPR on high-quality manikins to learning proper bandaging techniques, you’ll gain practical skills and confidence to respond in emergencies.

​

#### 3\. State & Employer Approved Courses

Our certifications meet Pennsylvania state requirements and are recognized by employers, schools, childcare organizations, and healthcare facilities. You’ll leave with the credentials you need, backed by training on industry-standard first aid supplies and tools.

​

#### 4\. Flexible Scheduling

We know life gets busy. That’s why we offer classes to fit your schedule, including evenings, weekends, and customized group sessions. Whether you're an individual learner or part of a team, we’ve got options tailored to your needs.
