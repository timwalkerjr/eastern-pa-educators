# Bloodborne Pathogen Training

## Why Bloodborne Pathogen Training is Essential

In today’s workplaces, especially in health care, it’s crucial to have a thorough understanding of bloodborne pathogens (BBPs) and how to handle potential exposure scenarios. Our training courses are designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills needed to protect yourself and others from the risks associated with bloodborne pathogens.

## What You'll Learn Through Our BBP & OPIM Classes

Our comprehensive training covers:

- Understanding Bloodborne Pathogens: Learn what BBPs are, how they spread, and the potential risks involved.

- Safety Precautions and Procedures: Get to know the best practices for handling blood and other potentially infectious materials (OPIM).

- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Discover how to choose, use, and dispose of PPE correctly.

- Exposure Control Plans: Develop and implement effective strategies to minimize exposure in the workplace.

- Emergency Response: Gain the confidence to respond effectively in the event of an exposure incident.


## Key Features of Our Blood Borne Pathogen Safety Courses

- Expert Instruction: Our courses are led by certified professionals with extensive experience in occupational health and safety.

- Interactive Learning: Engage with interactive modules, real-life scenarios, and hands-on activities that make learning practical and enjoyable.

- Flexible Scheduling: Choose from a variety of scheduling options to suit your needs.

- Certification: Upon successful completion, receive a certificate that demonstrates your commitment to workplace safety and compliance with OSHA standards.


## Who Should Attend Our Bloodborne Pathogen Training Sessions?

Our training courses are ideal for:

- Health care professionals

- Laboratory technicians

- First responders

- Janitorial and sanitation workers

- Employees in any industry where there is a risk of exposure to bloodborne pathogens
