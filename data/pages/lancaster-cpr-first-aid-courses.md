At Eastern PA Educators, we make CPR and first aid training simple, practical, and accessible for everyone in Lancaster. Whether you’re a teacher, childcare worker, healthcare professional, or just someone who wants to be prepared to save a life, our classes are designed to give you the skills and confidence to handle emergencies.

Emergencies can happen anytime, anywhere. Knowing what to do can save a life. At Eastern PA Educators, we’ll teach you how to perform CPR, use an AED, and provide first aid in a way that’s easy to understand and apply.

​

### Benefits of Choosing Eastern PA Educators for Your CPR & First Aid Training

​​

#### 1\. Learn from Experienced Instructors

Our certified trainers have hands-on emergency experience. They share real-world tips to help you stay calm and act confidently if an emergency happens.

​

#### 2\. Flexible Scheduling for Busy People

We know your time is valuable, so we offer evening, weekend, and group sessions to fit your schedule.

​

#### 3\. Certifications Employers Trust

Our courses are backed by the American Heart Association (AHA) and American Red Cross. They meet Pennsylvania state requirements and are accepted by employers, schools, and healthcare organizations.

​

#### 4\. Hands-On, Practical Training

You’ll practice key skills like CPR, using an AED, and performing first aid on lifelike manikins. Our classes are interactive, so you’ll feel confident applying what you’ve learned.
