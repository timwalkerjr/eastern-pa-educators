Eastern PA Educators is the go-to provider for CPR and first aid training in towns throughout Schuylkill County, PA including Pottsville, Tamaqua, Hometown, Orwigsburg, Coaldale, St. Clair, Port Clinton, McAdoo, New Ringgold, Minersville, Frackville, Schuylkill Haven, Lake Wynonah, Tower City, Valley View, Shenandoah, Tuscarora, Friedensburg, Mahanoy City, Pine Grove, Tremont, Blythe Township, West Penn Township, and surrounding communities.

​

Our instructors offer training for everyone, teachers, childcare workers, healthcare staff, or anyone who wants to learn how to save lives. Our courses give you the skills and confidence to act in emergencies when it matters most.

​

We know how important lifesaving skills are. Emergencies can happen anytime, anywhere. Knowing how to perform CPR, give first aid, or use an AED can make all the difference. Our training helps you take quick, effective action when needed.

​

### Why Choose Eastern PA Educators?

#### 1\. Hands-On Practice

We focus on learning by doing. You’ll practice CPR on manikins and learn how to use an AED, so you’re ready to handle real emergencies.

​

#### 2\. Experienced Instructors

Our certified trainers have years of real-life emergency experience. They’ll teach you the skills you need and how to handle unexpected situations with confidence.

​

#### 3\. Flexible Scheduling

We know life gets busy. That’s why we offer evening, weekend, and group sessions to fit your schedule.

​

#### 4\. Approved Certifications

Our courses meet Pennsylvania state rules and are accepted by employers, schools, hospitals, and childcare centers. With certifications from the American Heart Association (AHA) and the American Red Cross, you can trust you’re getting top-quality training.
