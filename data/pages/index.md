## 5 Reasons Learning First Aid is Important for Anyone to Know

1. First Aid Saves Lives. Based on a Red Cross survey, over half of deaths can be prevented if more people knew first aid.

2. Reduce Recovery Time.  Early intervention with proper first aid care can mean the difference between a quick or long recovery. Simple first aid techniques could keep an injury from becoming worse. For example, you can slow down the bleeding on a deep cut to prevent severe blood loss.

3. Prioritize Injuries.  First aid training will give you the knowledge needed to treat the most pressing injuries first.

4. Save a Trip to the Hospital.  You will know if a trip to the hospital is really necessary or if the injury would be better managed at home with proper bandaging and ice.

5. Keep Your Family Safe.  You will find a new sense of confidence once you have proper first aid training. You can keep your kids and even yourself safer with an increased sense of awareness and knowledge of first aid procedures.
